# Strings

Last updated: 2026-08-10

Treat a string as an indexed sequence with an explicit boundary invariant. Most
solutions are counting, matching, or shrinking a substring while keeping the
property that makes the next character safe to add.

Palindromes and substring questions belong here even when their implementation
uses a hash table or two pointers; the object being maintained is the string.
